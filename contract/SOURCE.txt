Generated by brain-edge's tools/http_manifest.py against its src/, which is
the edge's own definition of the HTTP/JSON contract. Refresh deliberately,
the same way the wire corpus is refreshed — never fetched in CI:

    python3 tools/http_manifest.py src > .../brain-sdk/contract/http-routes.json

It records every route (method, path, request body type, query type,
response type) and every DTO's fields with their JSON shapes and serde
attributes. The three SDK HTTP clients are checked against it.

A route may also carry `"streaming": "sse"`, which means its response is a
Server-Sent Events stream rather than a JSON body: `response` is then the
type of EACH event, the body never completes on its own, and a client needs
a reader rather than a deserializer. `GET /v1/subscribe` is the only one
today.

A route may instead carry `"streaming": "passthrough"`, meaning the body is
relayed to a third party unchanged and has no DTO either way. The broker's
two provider proxies are the only ones; their shapes are OpenAI's and
Anthropic's, not ours.

The parser reports anything it cannot read under "unparsed"; a manifest
with a non-empty unparsed list is not trustworthy. It is currently empty
across 60 routes, 123 DTOs and 5 enums.
