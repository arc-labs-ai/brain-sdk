PROVENANCE — reproducible pin.
This corpus is a verbatim copy of brain's committed conformance corpus at:

    111dd68b5221aad48023fe2a7cfb5fb1874c0b60

Verified reproducible: running brain's generator at that revision with
`BRAIN_CONFORMANCE_BLESS=1 cargo test -p brain-protocol --test conformance`
regenerates these exact bytes (zero diff). The bytes are the CBOR wire

COVERAGE (known gap, tracked separately).
The corpus pins a representative payload for the handshake, the cognitive
verbs, ENCODE_VECTOR_DIRECT, the *create* typed-graph responses
(resp_entity_create, resp_statement_create, resp_relation_create),
schema upload, QUERY, MATERIALIZE_PROCEDURAL, and the 9 error categories.
It does NOT yet pin the read-side response types — entity/statement GET,
LIST, RESOLVE, HISTORY, SUPERSEDE, TXN begin/commit/abort, LINK/UNLINK,
PLAN/REASON, SUBSCRIBE_EVENT, GET_CAPABILITIES, or the keepalive
PONG/SERVER_PING frames. Adding generator cases for those in brain's
`tests/conformance.rs` (then re-blessing + re-copying) is the corpus
expansion work; until then, byte-fidelity of those types across the
hand-written SDK ports is unverified.
